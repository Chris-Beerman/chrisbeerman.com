Addiction therapist in Chicago. Gambling, sports betting, high-risk trading, alcohol and substance use.

I run a private practice, Chicago Addiction Therapy, working with people whose gambling, drinking, or substance use has stopped being something they can talk themselves out of.

Most of the people I see aren't at rock bottom. They're functioning — holding down work, showing up for their families — and quietly aware that something has gotten away from them. That's a good time to start, not a reason to wait.

I see clients in person in Logan Square and online across Illinois and Minnesota. I also work with partners and family members, who often carry more of this than anyone acknowledges.

What I work with

  • Gambling and sports betting
  • High-risk trading and speculation
  • Alcohol and substance use
  • Partners and families affected by someone else's use
